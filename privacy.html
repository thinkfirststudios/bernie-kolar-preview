Privacy

This site is here so you can read about the peninsula and get in touch. It collects very little, and this page covers all of it.

What you send

The contact form asks for your name, an email or a phone number, whether you’re buying or selling, and a message. Only your name and one way to reach you are needed. The rest is up to you.

Where it goes

What you send goes to Bernie Kolar so she can reply to you. It isn’t sold or shared, and it isn’t added to a mailing list or a newsletter.

What this site doesn’t do

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising tags.
  • No chat widget.

The fonts and photographs load from this site, not from another company’s servers.

Questions, or removing what you sent

Email bernie@bluffsidere.com or call (920) 555-0137 to ask what she has from you or to have it deleted.
